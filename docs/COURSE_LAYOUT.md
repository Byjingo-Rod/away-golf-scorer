# Event-day Course Layout — Away 15.97.08 / Event Scorer 0.4.88

Live Event Control → Course Layout selects a published day. Replace a physical hole with a spare or with its own number for a shortened hole. Keep its playing position or insert it before/after a remaining physical hole, including before 1 or after 18. Replacement par is compulsory for all enabled playing tee/category cards. Length is optional and blank lengths do not block publication or scoring.

The original course snapshots and the shared master stay unchanged. A bounded command history derives the day-specific par/index/distance arrays. Stroke indexes inherit the removed hole's indexes unless a full, validated club allocation is supplied. Revised rating/slope can be entered; daily handicaps are recalculated where GA exists and explicitly reviewed before publication. Missing manual handicaps require entry.

The review displays playing position, physical hole and original Eclectic identity. Starts and NTPs are remapped and unresolved choices must be corrected. Existing NTPs must remain on revised par-3s. Original days retain their cards. Eclectic compares Stableford points using the original hole represented by each score, regardless of playing order. A modified Hole 14 remains Eclectic Hole 14.

Player scoring headings show a small physical-hole suffix where numbers differ and mark same-number modifications. Player acknowledgements are local and retained across refreshes. Score rows carry the acknowledged layout revision. Old clients/offline cards fail closed on a mismatch. A phone with older entries can retain its complete snapshot in the organiser's existing saved-copy review queue before opening a revised card; a failed retention cannot clear its local entries. Neither publication nor deployment rewrites saved scores.

Database triggers serialize score writes with layout updates, require an appended current layout revision, freeze started days and original cards in use, validate the derived card, selected categories, indexes, rating/slope, starts and NTPs. Existing authentication, customer approvals and course authority remain authoritative; no public RPC or new client table grants are added. Future days remain editable after earlier days are scored. Repeated/new events discard event-only layout overlays.

Verification: isolated JS/SQL equivalence; actual Eclectic leaderboard mapping; both real organiser dialog stages and local publication with blank length; signed handicaps and day-specific cards; Away trigger/mathematics rehearsal; GES tokened publication/scoring on later days, stale-client rejection and frozen scored layout.
