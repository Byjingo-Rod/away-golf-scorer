How to check this stage
  1. Choose a different view on each device. Press Got it on both player screens.
  2. Enter Alex’s score and Parker’s score on Alex’s phone. Enter the matching scores on Parker’s phone. Watch the leaderboard on your PC.
  3. Record a hole concession on one phone. Confirm it on the other then advance without entering scores.
  4. Turn one phone offline and enter a score. Reconnect. A pending entry stays on that device until saved. If another entry arrived first you must review it.
  5. Concede the match to test the finish. Confirm the result on both phones. The winner advances and the eliminated player retains leaderboard access.
